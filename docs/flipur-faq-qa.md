Question: What does Flipur actually do?
Answer: Flipur is a Chrome extension that flips your order side and type before the order is sent—for example buy limit at a price becomes sell stop at the same price. It is execution/psychological software, not financial advice.

Question: How many free flips do I get?

Question: Is Flipur safe with prop firms?
